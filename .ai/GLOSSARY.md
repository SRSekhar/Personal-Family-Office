# Glossary

## Principal

The owner of the Personal Family Office.

---

## PFO OS

Personal Family Office Operating System.

The software platform contained in this repository.

---

## Repository

The permanent source of truth for the project.

---

## Sprint

A focused implementation iteration delivering a defined set of capabilities.

---

## Epic

A major functional area consisting of multiple sprints.

---

## AI Foundation

The onboarding documents that enable any AI assistant to contribute consistently.

---

## Clean Architecture

Architectural style separating Presentation, Application, Domain and Data layers.

---

## Domain Driven Design (DDD)

An approach that models software around the business domain.

---

## Recommendation Engine

Component responsible for producing explainable financial recommendations.

---

## Analytics Engine

Component responsible for financial calculations and portfolio analytics.

---

## Principal Dashboard

The primary dashboard used by the Principal to review financial status.

---

## Investment Constitution

The long-term investment policy governing portfolio decisions.

---

## Financial Digital Twin

A structured digital representation of the Principal's financial life.

---

## PFO Score

A composite indicator representing overall financial health.

---

## Repository Health

A measure of documentation, architecture, automation and maintainability.

---

## Work Order

A discrete implementation task assigned to a sprint.

---

## Version

A semantic release of PFO OS following Semantic Versioning.
